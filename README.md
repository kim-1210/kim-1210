## 01 / About Me

I am an AI and robotics researcher interested in **connecting perception, learning, and robot control**.
I work on making robots understand their environment, learn useful behaviors, and perform reliable actions in the real world.

- **Research interests:** Reinforcement Learning, Imitation Learning, Robot Manipulation, Computer Vision
- **Robotics interests:** ROS 2, RGB-D Perception, Motion Planning, Sim-to-Real
- **Currently exploring:** Learning-based manipulation and perception-aware robot systems
